## Objective

This experiment estimates governance workflow costs and adoption dynamics for a DAO-like contract.

The pipeline uses:

- Etherscan-derived contract functions, events, and gas signals
- Dune-derived usage and trend signals
- human-reviewed MED abstractions
- human-reviewed probability modeling choices
- a backend-compatible simulation payload derived from approved artifacts

The experiment is intentionally staged so that each transition from evidence to abstraction is auditable.
